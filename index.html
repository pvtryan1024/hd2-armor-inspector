    Facing the enemy from the front: rear and hard-to-reach hitzones don't count. One-shot kills come first, sorted by damage (highest first); everything else is ranked by time to kill on its best frontal hitzone. Time comes from RPM with the first shot at 0 s, plus reloads (* = needs more than one magazine, reload time unknown). Weapons without an RPM are ranked by shot count after timed kills. Weapons that can't kill from the front go last, by their best time from any side.

    You won't always hit the weak spot. Facing the enemy from the front (rear and hard-to-reach hitzones don't count), this takes the fastest kill (best case: you can reach the weak points) and the slowest kill (worst case: you miss them). If the weapon can't damage some of those hitzones at all, the worst case is 4× the best case's shots. Real life leans to the worst case: 75% of the way from the best to the worst. Weapons that can't kill from the front are almost impossible and go last, by their best time from any side.
